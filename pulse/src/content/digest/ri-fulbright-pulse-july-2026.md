## Summer Briefing

The strongest pattern this summer is convergence: applicant advising, alumni engagement, and partner collaboration all connect through the same Rhode Island institutional nodes.

## Field Pathways to Use Now

### Public Health and Life Sciences

- URI advising and Brown pathways for applicants
- RI Bio, RI Life Science Hub, and NEMIC for collaboration and translation
- Public Health grantee signals available in the explorer

### Arts and Design

- RISD advising and RISD Museum cultural programming
- RISCA as a statewide arts sustainability node

### Policy and International Affairs

- Pell Center, Rhodes Center, and WACRI as policy/diplomacy conversation nodes

## Practical Actions by Audience

- **Applicants:** choose one advising node and one field pathway this month.
- **Alumni:** offer one mentorship intro or chapter referral.
- **Partners:** list one concrete Fulbright collaboration opportunity and submit it to Pulse.

## Event and Network Reminder

Event feeds remain distributed across chapter and partner channels. Pulse functions as the public map that points to current opportunities while preserving official chapter membership workflows.
