## Signal

Rhode Island institutions continue to post visible Fulbright outcomes, with high-profile signals from Brown, URI, and RISD.

## Why this matters statewide

This is more than campus prestige. It means Rhode Island has:

- advising systems that can raise applicant quality,
- alumni networks that can improve mentorship access,
- institutional credibility that helps partners engage.

## Alumni Opportunity Board

- **Mentor one applicant in your field (low effort):** 30-minute strategy call.
- **Offer one institutional introduction (medium effort):** connect applicant or alum to a relevant node.
- **Speak once this quarter (medium effort):** chapter, campus, or partner panel.

## One move this month

- **Applicants:** book one advising touchpoint and set draft milestones.
- **Alumni:** offer one mentorship conversation.
- **Partners:** submit one collaboration opportunity to Pulse.
