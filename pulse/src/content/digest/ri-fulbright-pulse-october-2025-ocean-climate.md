## Signal

URI oceanography has visible Fulbright-linked work across research, teaching, and global sustainability collaboration.

## Why it matters

Rhode Island can position itself as a strong coastal-climate exchange node, not just an applicant feeder.

## What this unlocks for audiences

- **Applicants:** stronger proposal feasibility through real institutional alignment.
- **Alumni:** pathways to contribute methods, policy, and fieldwork perspective.
- **Partners:** clearer route to collaboration topics with global relevance.

## One move this month

- **Applicants:** map your topic to one URI/RI climate institution.
- **Alumni:** offer one methods or fieldwork consult.
- **Partners:** post one climate/ocean collaboration invitation.
