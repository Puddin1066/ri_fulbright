## Signal

Official Fulbright pages reinforce the same pattern each cycle: early structure beats late effort.

## Why this matters

Rhode Island applicants often have strong ideas but uneven process timing. This is the month to close that gap.

## What strong preparation looks like

1. Pick one advising node and commit to a check-in schedule.
2. Narrow target countries to those that fit your profile and project scope.
3. Draft purpose and personal statement early enough for revision cycles.

## For Alumni

Alumni can raise applicant outcomes by sharing practical execution insights:

- statement revision tactics,
- realistic timeline lessons,
- field-specific framing mistakes to avoid.

## One move this month

- **Applicants:** map your timeline to campus + national milestones.
- **Alumni:** share one drafting lesson with one applicant.
- **Advisers/partners:** publish one clear support pathway.
