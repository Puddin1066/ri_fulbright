## Signal

Policy and international-economics institutions in Rhode Island provide recurring opportunities for Fulbright-informed public work.

## Why it matters

Applicants and alumni in policy fields need visible local channels where comparative global experience is useful.

## What stronger engagement looks like

- A policy-focused applicant can align project design with one Rhode Island institution before submission.
- An alum can convert Fulbright field experience into one local case briefing or workshop.
- A partner can publish one policy-facing collaboration ask with clear scope.

- **Applicants:** identify one policy node aligned with your intended country/field.
