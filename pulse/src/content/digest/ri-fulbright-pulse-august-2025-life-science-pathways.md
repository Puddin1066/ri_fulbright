## Signal

Rhode Island's life-science infrastructure is expanding with visible incubator and sector-level investment.

## Why this matters for Fulbright

This creates practical landing zones for:

- applicants in public health/biology,
- alumni seeking translational impact,
- partners looking for globally informed contributors.

## Opportunity map

- **Ocean State Labs / Portal** for early-stage company environment
- **RI Life Science Hub** for ecosystem and growth pathways
- **RI Bio / NEMIC** for network and applied innovation support

## For Alumni: Professional value

Instead of generic networking, use one concrete move:

- make one intro across researcher-founder-policy circles,
- join one field panel,
- co-define one pilot with a partner node.

## One move this month

- **Applicants:** identify one RI node aligned with your field.
- **Alumni:** initiate one applicant/partner introduction.
- **Partners:** publish one collaboration slot through Pulse.
