## Welcome

Rhode Island has hundreds of Fulbright grantees across decades of global work—but finding **local opportunities**, **campus advising**, and **chapter events** in one place has been hard. The official [Rhode Island chapter site](https://rhodeisland.fulbrightchapters.org/) remains the home for membership; **RI Fulbright Pulse** is the frequently updated public layer: resources, events, and searchable grantee history.

## By the numbers

Our explorer draws on public grant records for Rhode Island-affiliated grantees—from the postwar era through the 2025 cohort. Browse by field, country, or campus to see the shape of Fulbright in the Ocean State.

## Get involved

- Explore the **[Opportunity Atlas](/atlas)** for verified advising links at Brown, URI, RISD, and more.
- Check **[Events](/events)** for campus and chapter programming.
- **[Suggest a resource](/atlas#suggest)** so chapter volunteers can review and publish updates.

*Grantee names and project summaries on this site come from public Fulbright grant listings. Contact the chapter to request an update or removal.*
