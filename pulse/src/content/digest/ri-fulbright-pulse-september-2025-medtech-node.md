## Signal

Providence's medtech landscape is increasingly visible through incubators and partner organizations.

## Why this matters

Fulbright alumni with health, engineering, policy, and design backgrounds can provide high-value perspective in applied innovation settings.

## Where value can be created

- cross-cultural product/user insights,
- health system comparison for implementation strategy,
- policy and ethics framing for emerging health technologies.

## Alumni Opportunity Board

- **Low effort:** attend one ecosystem session and reconnect with chapter contacts.
- **Medium effort:** mentor one founder/applicant on global context.
- **Higher effort:** co-design one pilot concept with a partner institution.

## One move this month

- **Applicants:** understand innovation pathways beyond campus advising.
- **Alumni:** engage one medtech node.
- **Partners:** publish one applied project invitation.
