## This Month's Concept: From Awards to Ecosystem

A strong Fulbright network is not only about applications and awards. It is also about where returning scholars, applicants, and partners can collaborate locally.

This month, Pulse focuses on Rhode Island's ecosystem nodes with strong public visibility:

- innovation and commercialization pathways
- international visitor and diplomacy pathways
- cultural and civic exchange pathways
