## Signal

Rhode Island has durable institutions for diplomacy-facing dialogue and international visitor exchange.

## Why it matters

Fulbright value grows when alumni and institutions use civic platforms to sustain exchange after grant periods end.

## Engagement opportunities

- Join one public-facing global affairs event.
- Contribute one practical session, panel, or workshop idea.
- Connect one applicant/alum to an institution hosting international dialogue.

## One move this month

- **Applicants:** attend one public global-affairs event.
- **Alumni:** contribute one speaker/session proposal.
- **Partners:** host one collaboration conversation with international or cross-sector participants.
