## Signal

Rhode Island's arts and culture institutions can serve as durable channels for international exchange beyond formal grant periods.

## Why it matters

Applicants and alumni in arts, design, museum studies, and public humanities need clear institutional nodes for local engagement.

## What engaging participation can look like

- arts-focused applicants map proposal context to one RI institution,
- alumni host one public-facing exchange conversation,
- partner institutions define one collaboration opening discoverable through Pulse.

## One move this month

- **Applicants:** map your proposal to one RI cultural institution context.
- **Alumni:** propose one exchange-facing event, talk, or workshop.
- **Partners:** add one discoverable collaboration opportunity to Pulse.

Reference nodes:

- [RISCA](https://arts.ri.gov/)
- [RISD Museum](https://risdmuseum.org/)
