## Signal Check: Why This Month Matters

Recent high-visibility updates from official Fulbright and Rhode Island institutions suggest two things:

1. Applicant competition is active and early preparation matters.
2. Rhode Island institutions remain strong contributors to Fulbright outcomes.

## Official Program Context

From official U.S. Student Program pages:

- Fulbright's U.S. Student cycle is open and timeline-driven.
- Campus advising remains the best first step for most applicants.

- [Fulbright U.S. Student Program](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/fulbright-us-student-program)

Brown has sustained top-tier Fulbright production in recent years, including top national rankings and large annual award cohorts.
